# Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 16, 2026

sessionhue (the command line tool and Mac notifier) and the Sessionhue app for iPhone and Apple Watch do not collect any data.

## What stays with you

- **No accounts, no analytics, no tracking, no ads.** We do not run a server, and nothing is sent to us or to anyone else.
- **Notifications go through your own iCloud.** When you turn on notifications for iPhone or Apple Watch, the Mac stores each waiting notification (session title, text and possible answers) in the private iCloud database of your own Apple Account. Your iPhone and Apple Watch read it from there, and your answer goes back the same way. Only you can access this data. The Mac deletes each notification once it is answered or settled.
- **On your devices only:** the list of sessions and your quick replies are stored on your iPhone.
- **Apple services:** Apple delivers the notifications and stores the iCloud data under Apple's own [privacy policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/).
